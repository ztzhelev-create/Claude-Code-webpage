A full cupboard is not the same as a useful one. I have bought the specialist
vinegar. It is still there, three years on, behind the lentils I actually use.

What follows is what survived — the things that earn their shelf space because
they turn up in something most weeks.

Red lentils collapse into a soup in twenty minutes without soaking, which makes
them the fastest hot meal in the house. Tinned beans do the same job with even
less effort. Smoked paprika does more for a pan of onions than any fresh herb.
Bay leaves last for years and quietly improve anything simmered.

Good olive oil is the one place to spend money, because it is the only thing on
the list you will taste undisguised.

The rest is upkeep. Buy the small jar of a spice you have never used, not the
large one. Keep the things you reach for at the front. And accept that the
specialist vinegar was always going to be a mistake.
