This is a weekend dish, and it does not want to be hurried. The orange is not a
garnish — it is the thing that stops three hours of pork from becoming heavy.

## Ingredients

* 500g dried black beans, soaked overnight
* 300g smoked pork ribs
* 200g smoked sausage, thickly sliced
* 200g pork shoulder, cubed
* 2 onions, finely chopped
* 6 garlic cloves, crushed
* 2 bay leaves
* 1 tsp black pepper
* Salt
* To serve: white rice, flat-leaf parsley, 2 oranges in slices

## Method

1. Drain the soaked beans and put them in a large pot with the ribs, bay leaves
   and enough cold water to cover by three fingers. Bring to a boil, then lower to
   a steady simmer.
2. Cook for 1 hour, skimming the foam off the top in the first ten minutes.
3. Brown the sausage and pork shoulder in a frying pan, then add them to the pot
   with any fat left behind.
4. In the same pan, soften the onions and garlic, then stir a ladleful of beans
   into them, mash the lot to a paste and return it to the pot. This is what
   thickens the stew.
5. Simmer gently for another 1½ hours, until the beans give completely under
   light pressure and the liquid has gone glossy and dark.
6. Season with salt and pepper. Serve over rice with parsley on top and orange
   slices on the side.
