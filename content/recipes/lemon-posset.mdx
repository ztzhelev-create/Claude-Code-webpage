Posset sets because lemon juice curdles hot cream into something silken. There is
no gelatine and no cooking beyond a brief boil, so the only way to get it wrong
is to under-boil the cream.

## Ingredients

* 600ml double cream
* 150g caster sugar
* 3 lemons: all the juice, zest of 1
* For the shortbread: 125g butter, softened
* 55g caster sugar
* 180g plain flour
* 1 tbsp thyme leaves
* A pinch of salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Beat the butter and sugar together, then work in the
   flour, thyme and salt until it comes together as a stiff dough.
2. Press into a lined tin about 2cm thick, prick all over, and bake for 30
   minutes until pale gold. Cut into fingers while still hot, then cool in the tin.
3. Put the cream and sugar in a pan and bring to a full rolling boil. Let it boil
   hard for exactly 3 minutes, stirring, and watch that it does not climb the pan.
4. Take it off the heat and stir in the lemon juice and zest. It will thicken
   visibly within seconds.
5. Pour into six small glasses and chill for at least 3 hours, until set firm
   enough to hold the mark of a spoon.
6. Serve cold, with the shortbread alongside.
