Scoring the cut side matters more than anything else here. It lets the heat into
the middle so the flesh turns to custard instead of staying squeaky.

## Ingredients

* 2 large aubergines, halved lengthways
* 3 tbsp neutral oil
* 3 tbsp white miso paste
* 2 tbsp mirin
* 1 tbsp maple syrup
* 1 tbsp rice vinegar
* 1 tsp grated ginger
* 2 spring onions, sliced thin
* 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Score the cut face of each aubergine half in a
   diamond pattern, cutting deep but not through the skin.
2. Brush generously with oil, season, and roast cut side up for 25 minutes,
   until the flesh is soft enough to take a spoon without resistance.
3. While they roast, whisk the miso, mirin, maple syrup, vinegar and ginger into
   a smooth, pourable glaze.
4. Spread the glaze over the cut faces and return to the oven under a hot grill
   for 4 to 5 minutes, watching closely — miso burns quickly once it starts.
5. Scatter with spring onions and sesame seeds and serve hot, with rice.
