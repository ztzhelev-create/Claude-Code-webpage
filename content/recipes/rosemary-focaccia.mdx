The dough should feel too wet to handle. That is the point: the water becomes
steam in the oven, and the steam is what opens up the crumb.

## Ingredients

* 500g strong white bread flour
* 400ml lukewarm water
* 7g fast-action dried yeast
* 10g fine salt
* 6 tbsp olive oil, plus more for the tin
* 2 sprigs rosemary, leaves picked
* Flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Stir the flour, yeast and fine salt together. Pour in the water and 2 tbsp of
   the oil and mix with a spoon until no dry flour remains. It will be sticky and
   shaggy; do not knead it.
2. Cover and leave at room temperature for 2 hours, folding the dough over itself
   four times at the half-hour mark and again an hour in.
3. Oil a deep baking tin generously. Tip the dough in, turn it once to coat, and
   leave uncovered for a further 1½ hours until pillowy.
4. Heat the oven to 220°C. Oil your fingers and press them straight down through
   the dough to the base of the tin, all over, to make the dimples.
5. Scatter over the rosemary and flaky salt, drizzle with the remaining oil, and
   bake for 25 minutes until deep gold at the edges.
6. Lift it out of the tin onto a rack straight away, or the base will steam and
   soften. Eat warm.
