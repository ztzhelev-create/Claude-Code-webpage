The sauce wants longer than you think. Peppers cooked for fifteen minutes taste
sharp; cooked for twenty-five they turn sweet, and the whole dish changes.

## Ingredients

* 3 tbsp olive oil
* 1 onion, sliced
* 2 red peppers, sliced into strips
* 3 garlic cloves, sliced
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp sweet paprika
* 400g tin chopped tomatoes
* 1 tsp sugar
* 4 eggs
* 100g feta, broken into pieces
* A small bunch of dill, roughly chopped
* Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Warm the oil in a wide frying pan. Add the onion and peppers with a good pinch
   of salt and cook over a medium heat for 20 minutes, until completely soft and
   beginning to colour.
2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and stir for a minute.
3. Tip in the tomatoes and sugar, season, and simmer for 10 minutes until the
   sauce no longer runs when you draw a spoon through it.
4. Make four hollows in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover the pan and
   cook for 5 to 7 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks still move.
5. Scatter over the feta and dill. Serve from the pan, with bread to collect
   everything left behind.
