The smoke here comes from the pan, not the tin. Get the onions darker than feels
sensible and the stew will taste like it had a much longer day than it did.

## Ingredients

* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 large onion, sliced thin
* 4 garlic cloves, crushed
* 2 tsp smoked paprika
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tbsp tomato purée
* 400g tin chopped tomatoes
* 2 x 400g tins borlotti or cannellini beans, drained
* 250ml chicken or vegetable stock
* 1 bay leaf
* A handful of coriander, leaves picked
* Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a heavy pan over a medium-high heat. Add the onion and leave it
   alone for a few minutes at a time, stirring only when it starts to catch, until
   it is deeply browned at the edges — around 12 minutes.
2. Turn the heat down. Add the garlic, paprika and cumin and stir for a minute,
   until the spices smell toasted rather than raw.
3. Stir in the tomato purée and cook it out for two minutes, until it darkens.
4. Add the tomatoes, beans, stock and bay leaf. Season well.
5. Simmer uncovered for 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the sauce is
   thick enough to hold the mark of a spoon.
6. Taste, adjust the salt, and scatter the coriander over just before serving.
