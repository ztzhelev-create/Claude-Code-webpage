Tahini sets firm when it is cold, which means fudge without a thermometer or a
sugar stage. Use a runny tahini you would happily eat from the jar.

## Ingredients

* 250g runny tahini, well stirred
* 120ml maple syrup or honey
* A pinch of fine salt
* 200g dark chocolate, chopped
* 1 tbsp sesame seeds
* Flaky sea salt, to finish

## Method

1. Line a small loaf tin with baking paper, leaving an overhang on two sides.
2. Stir the tahini, syrup and fine salt together until the mixture thickens and
   pulls away from the spoon. It will look split before it comes together — keep
   going.
3. Scrape it into the tin, level the top, and freeze for 2 hours until firm.
4. Lift the slab out and cut it into squares. Return them to the freezer while you
   melt the chocolate in short bursts.
5. Dip each square halfway, letting the excess run off, and set them on paper.
6. Scatter sesame seeds and flaky salt over while the chocolate is still wet.
   Chill for 10 minutes to set. Keep them in the fridge.
