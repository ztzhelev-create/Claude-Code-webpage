The soup itself is almost plain. Everything that makes it worth eating happens in
the last minute: hot paprika butter poured over, and more lemon than feels right.

## Ingredients

* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 onion, chopped
* 1 carrot, chopped
* 1 tbsp tomato purée
* 250g red lentils, rinsed
* 1.2 litres vegetable or chicken stock
* 1 tsp dried mint
* Salt and black pepper
* For the butter: 40g butter, 1 tsp sweet paprika, a pinch of chilli flakes
* 2 lemons, cut into wedges

## Method

1. Soften the onion and carrot in the oil over a medium heat for 8 minutes,
   without letting them colour.
2. Stir in the tomato purée and cook for a minute, then add the lentils, stock
   and dried mint.
3. Simmer for 25 minutes, until the lentils have broken down completely and the
   soup has thickened.
4. Blend until entirely smooth — this soup should have no texture at all. Loosen
   with hot water if it has gone claggy, and season well.
5. Melt the butter in a small pan, take it off the heat and stir in the paprika
   and chilli. It should foam and turn deep orange.
6. Ladle the soup out, pour a spoonful of the butter over each bowl, and serve
   with lemon wedges. Tell people to use both.
